📄 Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Project: Hardware Store POS + Online Catalog
Version: 1.0
Type: Web-based (Laravel + MySQL on cPanel)
Scope: Single Shop, Single Cashier, Online Catalog (No Checkout)
1. 🎯 Product Overview
1.1 Purpose

Build a web-based Point of Sale (POS) system integrated with a public online catalog for a local hardware and sanitary store.

The system will:

Handle in-store billing

Manage inventory

Track purchases and stock

Provide basic sales reports

Display products online (catalog only, no checkout)

2. 🏪 Business Context

Store Type: Hardware & Sanitary

Counters: 1

Cashiers: 1

Branches: 1

Online Orders: ❌ No

Delivery: ❌ No

Payments: Cash / Card (manual entry)

Hosting: cPanel Shared Hosting

Technology: Laravel + MySQL

3. 🎯 Goals & Objectives
Primary Goals

Fast and simple POS billing

Non-Goals
